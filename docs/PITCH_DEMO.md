# Pitch, video & live demo

Deliverables (BRIEF §8): **slides ≤ 5 min**, **video ≤ 2 min** (one real call end to end), **live demo** where the
jury calls our agent with its own scenarios. Judged on smart / fast / natural + relevance of the problem.

| 4:30–5:00 | What's next | what we'd ship next week; thanks |

Rule: only measured numbers on slides. Say how they were measured (t0 = end of turn, typed vs mic).

alternative → read-back → confirmation → the order visible in the ERP. Record two takes at 13:00; keep the best.
